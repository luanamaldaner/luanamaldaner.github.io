DataFables

3rd Place — Entertainment Track · Hacklytics 2026

Turns any topic into a personalized illustrated kids' storybook with branching stories, vocab learning, and narration in 10 languages.

The Problem

Most educational screen time for kids is passive and generic. Children need interactive, personalized experiences that build literacy, creativity, and language skills simultaneously, without sacrificing engagement.

The Solution

DataFables is an interactive storytelling platform where kids choose a genre, create a custom protagonist, and make choices that shape a uniquely generated illustrated story. Stories are calibrated to Lexile reading levels by age, offer pronunciation practice in 10 languages, and reward progress with XP and in-game currency for character customization.

Features

  • Customizable Stories: Choose from 6 genres, design your protagonist, and make branching narrative choices
  • Age-Appropriate Content: Stories match Lexile reading levels based on the reader's age range
  • Language Learning: Pronunciation practice with instant feedback across 10 languages
  • Reward System: Earn XP and in-game currency through story completion for character customization
  • Global Accessibility: Fully multilingual interface and story generation

My Contributions

I built the Snowflake integration that powers the knowledge base and implemented the achievements system, including XP tracking and reward logic tied to story completion milestones.
